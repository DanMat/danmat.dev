# Design brief — for a Fable art-direction pass (WIP)

> Hand this to a creative/design model (Fable) when the real build starts. Goal: a bold,
> distinctive visual identity for danmat.dev — **not** a generic dev-portfolio template.
> Draft — refine with Dan before running the pass. Gated on Nimbus CMS being ready.

## The person (internalize this)

Dan Matthew is a **dev-tools & platform builder — "an app orchestra."** He finds gaps and
builds apps to close them. Pre-AI he hand-wrote ~48 internal tools (scaffolders, crawlers,
CI tooling) at Dealer.com; now he orchestrates AI to ship whole ecosystems (a game engine +
arcade, travel-map tools, an audiobook retrospective, a scaffolder, a CMS). He's transparent
about building with AI and believes the craft is shifting from *writing* code to *composing*
systems. The site is his business card for **this** moment.

## What it must feel like

- **Uniquely him** — a business card, not a resume. Memorable in three seconds.
- **"App orchestra"** as the central metaphor: one conductor, many instruments/apps playing
  together. Explore it literally or abstractly.
- Carries an **arcade / synthwave soul** — the current WIP "insert coin" screen (flyable
  ship, CRT scanlines, neon synthwave grid) is a real signal — but matured into something
  that reads as *considered*, not a template.
- Confident and current — comfortable in the AI era **without** the "AI-generated purple
  gradient" cliché.
- **A whole person, not a résumé.** The work spans real obsessions — games, travel,
  audiobooks, a 100k-stamp collection — because he builds an app for whatever he's into.
  The design should let personality and passions show through, not read as a corporate deck.

## Concrete asks

1. **2–3 distinct art directions** — each with mood, palette (4–6 hexes), type pairing,
   layout concept, and one signature interaction/motion idea.
2. A **hero concept** that states the thesis in a single glance.
3. A way to present the **project gallery** as *instruments in an orchestra / a control
   surface / a system* — not a boring card grid.
4. How the **"powered by my own CMS (Nimbus)"** story surfaces visually.
5. **Motion/interactivity** direction — keep a playable/interactive soul (the current screen
   already lets you fly a ship around a synthwave grid).

## Hard constraints

- **Distinctive** — explicitly avoid generic AI-portfolio defaults (cream + serif +
  terracotta; lone neon-on-black; purple→blue gradient hero; Inter as the "safe" face;
  everything centered and `rounded-lg`).
- Theme-aware, responsive, accessible, `prefers-reduced-motion`-friendly.
- Buildable as a **packkit react-app on Cloudflare**, content from **Nimbus CMS**.
- Keep it *him*: playful, builder-brained, honest.

## Still open (bring options, don't assume)

- Overall tone: pure arcade vs. arcade-meets-editorial vs. something new.
- How much to foreground the AI-era thesis vs. let the work speak for itself.
